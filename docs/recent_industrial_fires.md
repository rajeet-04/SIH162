## Use notes

- These are **news-reported incidents**, not a complete official national register.
- Casualty counts and causes are recorded as reported at the time of publication; they should be rechecked before use in a formal incident database.
- For a thermal-detection pipeline, the Haldia, Rewari and Faridabad incidents are the strongest candidates for cross-checking against satellite acquisition windows because the reports describe substantial fires or explosions at industrial sites.
- The Hapur, Ghaziabad and Panchkula reports are useful recent examples, but the public reporting provides limited geospatial precision and limited confirmed damage measurements.
